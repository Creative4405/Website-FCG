Privacy Notice

How Future Creative Group protects and uses portal information.

POPIA

Purpose and lawful processing

We process client identity, contact, project, billing, support and document information only to deliver contracted services, maintain business records, provide support and meet legal obligations.

Access and security

Portal information is limited by role and client account. Access, document downloads and important administrative actions are logged. Secure sessions, protected uploads and expiring document links reduce unauthorised access.

Retention and your rights

Business and financial records are retained only for the applicable service, contractual and statutory periods. You may request correction, access or deletion where legally permitted by contacting the Admin Department.

Information Officer submissions

Privacy, POPIA and PAIA requests should be sent to admin@futurecreativegroup.co.za.

Portal Terms of Use

Rules for secure access to Future Creative Group business records.

Authorised use

The portal is provided to authorised clients and staff for project, quotation, invoice, payment, support and document administration. Access credentials must be kept private and must not be shared with unauthorised persons.

Records and approvals

Portal records support service delivery and administration. Formal quotations, invoices, receipts and signed agreements remain the controlling business documents.

Security

Users must report suspected unauthorised access promptly. Future Creative Group may suspend access to protect client information, investigate misuse or maintain the service.
