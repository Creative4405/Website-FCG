Privacy and POPIA Notice

This notice explains how Future Creative Group Pty Ltd processes personal information when visitors request quotations, use contact forms, access the client portal, receive invoices, or use hosting and service support.

Information collected

Future Creative Group may collect personal and business information that a person submits directly, information required to quote or deliver services, and technical records created during website, hosting, support or portal use.

  • Names, company names, contact numbers, email addresses and service addresses.
  • Project, site, support, technical and service requirement details.
  • Quotation, invoice, payment, proof-of-payment and account administration records.
  • Portal login identifiers, access records, uploaded documents and support requests.
  • Hosting, domain, email, DNS, SSL, troubleshooting and service logs where needed to deliver support.

Purpose of processing

Personal information is processed for legitimate business purposes, contract preparation and performance, legal and tax recordkeeping, security, support, service delivery and communication with clients or prospective clients.

Quotation and contact forms

When a visitor submits a quotation, sign-up or contact form, Future Creative Group uses the submitted details to respond, assess requirements, prepare pricing, schedule work, provide follow-up communication and keep enquiry records.

Client portal information

The client portal may process client profiles, project updates, quotations, invoices, receipts, uploaded documents, payment records, support tickets and activity logs. Portal access is intended only for authorised users issued with access details.

Invoices, payment records, hosting and service records

Future Creative Group may retain billing, tax, payment and service records to administer client accounts, confirm work performed, manage disputes, support warranty or service queries, and comply with applicable South African recordkeeping duties.

Information sharing

Information may be shared with staff, contractors, suppliers, hosting providers, domain registrars, payment or accounting providers, courier or installation partners, professional advisers, regulators or authorities where required for service delivery, legal compliance, security or legitimate business operations.

Security safeguards

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be applied, including access controls, secure portal sessions, limited document access, careful supplier selection, backup controls where applicable, and internal procedures for handling sensitive client records.

Data retention

Records should be kept only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, for active services, for statutory recordkeeping periods, for dispute handling, or for legitimate business requirements. Final retention periods must be confirmed by the owner or legal adviser.

Data subject rights

Data subjects may have rights under POPIA to request access to personal information, request correction or deletion where legally permitted, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and complain to the Information Regulator.

Access, correction and deletion requests

Requests should be submitted to the Admin Department at admin@futurecreativegroup.co.za. Future Creative Group may need to verify the requester's identity before disclosing, correcting or deleting information.

Information Officer and Information Regulator complaint information

POPIA and Information Officer submissions: admin@futurecreativegroup.co.za.

Complaints may be submitted to the Information Regulator (South Africa) using the regulator's official channels.
