Legal & Compliance

Service Terms and Conditions

These terms explain how Future Creative Group services are quoted, approved, delivered and supported. They should be read with the accepted quotation, invoice, service agreement or project proposal.

Department contacts

Service and quotation enquiries should be sent to sales@futurecreativegroup.co.za. Billing and payment queries should be sent to account@futurecreativegroup.co.za. Technical support should be sent to support@futurecreativegroup.co.za.

Quotation validity

Quotations are valid for the period stated on the quotation. If no period is stated, validity must be confirmed in writing before acceptance. Pricing may change because of supplier pricing, exchange rates, stock availability, VAT, travel, site conditions or changed client requirements.

Deposits and payment

Deposits, progress payments and final balances are payable as stated on the quotation or invoice. Work may be scheduled only once required deposits, signed approvals or purchase orders have been received. Payment confirmations and account queries should be sent to the Accounts Department.

Project scope

The scope of work is limited to the items listed in the accepted quotation, proposal or service agreement. Anything not expressly included may be quoted separately, including travel, extra hardware, civil work, electrical work, licences, third-party subscriptions, content, copywriting or complex integrations.

Client responsibilities

  • Provide accurate requirements, access, approvals, content, credentials and contact details.
  • Ensure site readiness, safe working access and required permissions.
  • Review deliverables and respond within agreed review periods.
  • Keep backups of client-owned information unless backup services are expressly included.

Timelines

Timelines are estimates unless expressly confirmed as fixed in writing. Delays may occur because of late client information, supplier delays, weather, access restrictions, connectivity issues, payment delays, regulatory requirements or events outside reasonable control.

Scope changes

Changes requested after approval may affect pricing, timelines and deliverables. Future Creative Group may require written approval and payment before carrying out additional work.

Third-party suppliers

Some services rely on third-party suppliers such as hosting platforms, domain registrars, device manufacturers, networks, software vendors, couriers, payment providers or advertising platforms. Supplier terms, availability, warranties and service levels may apply.

Domain and hosting services

Website hosting, business email, domains, DNS, SSL and cloud services may be delivered through FCG Cloud or other approved suppliers. The client remains responsible for renewal approvals, accurate registrant details, acceptable use and payment of recurring services.

Cancellations

Cancellation rights, refund eligibility and cancellation fees depend on the approved quotation, work already performed, supplier commitments, ordered goods, custom items, licences and recurring service terms. Cancellation requests should be submitted in writing to the department handling the service.

Non-payment and suspension

If amounts remain unpaid after the due date, Future Creative Group may pause work, withhold deliverables, suspend portal access, suspend recurring services, charge permitted recovery costs or require payment before reactivation, subject to applicable law and written agreements.

Data and backups

Clients should maintain their own backups unless a backup service is expressly included. Future Creative Group is not responsible for loss caused by missing client backups, incorrect credentials, malware, third-party outages, unauthorised access caused by client-side security failures or expired services.

Warranty limitations

Manufacturer warranties, workmanship warranties and service warranties are subject to inspection, supplier terms, normal use, proper maintenance and the Warranty and Returns Policy. Misuse, unauthorised changes, accidental damage, power surges, environmental damage and third-party interference may void warranty support.

Disputes and governing law

The parties should first try to resolve disputes through written communication and escalation to management. These terms are governed by South African law unless a signed agreement states otherwise.
