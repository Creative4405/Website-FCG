Warranty and Returns Policy

This policy separates manufacturer warranty, Future Creative Group workmanship warranty, hardware returns, custom-order items, software and licences, accidental damage and inspection processes.

Warranty and returns contacts

Warranty, returns and inspection requests should be submitted to support@futurecreativegroup.co.za. Refund, credit or account queries should be submitted to account@futurecreativegroup.co.za.

Manufacturer warranty

Hardware such as CCTV cameras, recorders, routers, switches, access control devices, intercoms, VoIP phones and accessories may carry a manufacturer or supplier warranty. Warranty periods, exclusions, repair processes and replacement decisions are controlled by the applicable manufacturer or supplier terms.

Future Creative Group workmanship warranty

Future Creative Group may provide a workmanship warranty for installation or service work performed by its team, subject to inspection, normal use and the specific quotation or service agreement. The workmanship warranty does not replace manufacturer warranties or cover damage outside Future Creative Group's workmanship.

Hardware returns

Hardware returns are subject to supplier approval, inspection, condition, packaging, proof of purchase and applicable consumer law. Opened, installed, used or damaged items may not qualify for return unless required by law or approved by the supplier.

Custom-order items

Custom-order, special-order, project-specific or imported items may be non-returnable once ordered, unless defective or returnable under supplier terms or applicable law. Deposits for custom goods may be non-refundable where supplier commitments have already been made.

Software and licences

Software, digital services, hosting, domains, SSL certificates, email accounts, subscriptions, activation keys and licences may be non-refundable after activation, provisioning, registration or delivery, subject to supplier terms and applicable law.

Misuse or accidental damage

Warranty or return approval may be refused where damage is caused by misuse, incorrect installation by others, power surges, lightning, water, fire, theft, vandalism, tampering, unauthorised repairs, malware, poor maintenance, environmental exposure or accidental damage.

Call-out fees

Call-out, labour, travel, diagnostic or collection fees may apply where inspection shows that the issue is not covered by warranty, is caused by misuse or third-party interference, or requires onsite support outside the agreed warranty scope. Applicable fees will be communicated before non-warranty work proceeds where reasonably possible.

Inspection and approval process

  1. The client reports the fault with invoice or project reference details.
  2. Future Creative Group may request photos, serial numbers, login details, site access or diagnostic information.
  3. The item or installation may be inspected by Future Creative Group, the supplier or the manufacturer.
  4. Warranty, repair, replacement, credit or return approval is confirmed after inspection.
  5. No credit, refund or replacement is guaranteed before approval is issued in writing.
