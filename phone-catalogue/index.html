Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for clients comparing Fanvil VoIP phones, hosted PBX devices and setup requirements.

Do these phones work with hosted PBX?

Yes. These Fanvil VoIP phones are suitable for hosted PBX and business VoIP environments, depending on the selected service provider and configuration.

Can Future Creative Group configure the phones?

Yes. We can quote device configuration, hosted PBX setup, extensions, call routing and related support based on your requirements.

Are prices including VAT?

Yes. Retail device prices shown include VAT. Delivery, installation, PBX setup, number porting and configuration are quoted separately.

Can I request a bulk quote?

Yes. You can add multiple devices to the Quote Basket and submit a website quotation request.

Can I still request a quote via WhatsApp?

Yes. Each product includes a WhatsApp quote option, and the page also supports website quote requests.

Do I need internet for VoIP phones?

Yes. VoIP phones require a stable internet connection and proper network setup for reliable call quality.

Do you help with number porting?

Yes. Number porting assistance can be quoted depending on the client's current number provider and requirements.
