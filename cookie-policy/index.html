Cookie Policy

This policy explains cookies and similar storage used by the Future Creative Group website and client portal. A consent banner should only be shown when non-essential analytics, Meta Pixel or marketing scripts are active.

On this pageEssential cookiesPortal cookiesAnalytics cookiesMarketing cookiesThird-party servicesCookie retentionControl preferencesConsent banner rule
Cookie enquiries

Cookie and privacy enquiries should be sent to admin@futurecreativegroup.co.za. A consent banner is required only when non-essential analytics, Meta Pixel or marketing scripts are active.

Essential cookies and local storage

Essential cookies and local storage may be used to keep the website functional, remember display preferences such as dark or light mode, support quote basket behaviour and protect forms or portal sessions.

Authentication and client portal cookies

The client portal uses authentication/session cookies to keep authorised users signed in, protect access, maintain security tokens and support role-based records. These are necessary for secure portal functionality.

Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies may be used only if analytics tools are enabled. These tools help understand website traffic, page performance and enquiry behaviour. If analytics cookies are activated, the site should show a non-essential cookie consent banner before they run.

Marketing cookies

Marketing cookies, pixels and tags, including Meta Pixel or advertising conversion tracking, should only be used when configured for a campaign and when a compliant consent mechanism is active before those scripts load.

Third-party services

The website may link to or embed third-party services such as Google Maps, Google reviews, WhatsApp, social media platforms, FCG Cloud, payment providers or official regulator websites. Those services may apply their own cookies and privacy terms when visitors interact with them.

Cookie retention

Essential session cookies may expire when the browser session ends or after a security timeout. Preference storage may remain until cleared by the visitor. Analytics and marketing cookie retention must be confirmed from the relevant provider before those tools are enabled.

How visitors can control preferences

  • Use browser settings to block or delete cookies and local storage.
  • Use browser privacy controls or private browsing where appropriate.
  • Use the site's consent banner controls if non-essential cookies are enabled in future.
  • Understand that blocking essential cookies may affect portal login, forms or preference features.

Consent banner rule

Do not show a cookie consent banner for essential cookies only. Show a clear opt-in consent banner before non-essential analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising, remarketing or marketing scripts are activated.
