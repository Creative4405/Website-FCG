Pricing Factors

What Affects Network Pricing

Final pricing depends on the site layout, equipment and installation complexity.

Coverage area

Larger or multi-floor sites may need more access points and testing.

Cable routes

Long runs, ceiling work and trunking can affect labour and materials.

Equipment choice

Routers, switches, access points and cabinets are quoted based on requirements.

Security needs

Guest networks, VLAN guidance and firewall work may need additional scope.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you request a package quote.

Can you improve weak Wi-Fi coverage?

Yes. We can assess coverage and quote access point placement, configuration and testing.

Do you install network points?

Yes. Structured cabling and network point installation can be quoted per point.

Is equipment included in the price?

Equipment is quoted separately unless clearly included in a written quotation.

Can you connect printers and office devices?

Yes. Printer and network device connection can be included in office network setup.

Can networking be combined with CCTV or VoIP?

Yes. Networking often supports CCTV, VoIP, access control and IT support requirements.
