Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you request a package quote.

Do you manage Facebook and Instagram?

Yes. Facebook and Instagram support is included across the marketing packages, with management level depending on the selected package.

Is ad budget included?

No. Advertising budget is not included in monthly package pricing and is quoted separately based on campaign goals.

Do you create captions?

Yes. Captions are included, from basic captions on starter plans to fuller support on higher plans.

Can you help with WhatsApp marketing?

Yes. We can create WhatsApp status and promotional content for direct customer updates.

Can you design promotional flyers?

Yes. Promotional flyer designs are included in monthly packages and can also be quoted separately.

Can digital marketing be combined with website design?

Yes. Marketing can be combined with website design, landing pages, hosting and branding services.
