Pricing Factors

What Affects Pricing

Access control pricing depends on hardware, cabling, integration and number of entry points.

Entry points

Multiple gates, doors or zones require more hardware and setup time.

Lock type

Magnetic locks, electric locks, exit buttons and power supplies affect scope.

Cabling and network

Cable routing, network equipment and power availability can change pricing.

Visitor flow

Advanced visitor management and reporting may need custom software or hardware.
