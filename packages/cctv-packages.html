Recommendation

Best CCTV Package Recommendation

Use this as a starting point before a site assessment confirms camera placement, cable distance and storage needs.

Home / small shop

Start with a 4CH package for basic entrance, till, reception or small-premises coverage.

Business premises

An 8CH package is better for multiple areas such as entrances, counters, storage and parking.

Larger premises

A 16CH package supports wider zone coverage and more viewing points.

Enterprise / site

A 32CH or custom package is recommended for multi-zone, multi-user or larger premises.

Planning Factors

What Affects CCTV Pricing?

Final CCTV pricing depends on site layout, camera count, cabling and remote-viewing requirements.

Cable distance

Longer runs and difficult routes can affect labour and material costs.

Camera quality

Resolution, night vision, weather rating and analytics affect device pricing.

Storage requirements

Recording duration and camera count influence hard-drive and recorder sizing.

Monitor requirements

On-site display monitors are quoted separately where needed.

Internet / router access

Remote viewing depends on network availability and configuration.

Height and accessibility

High or difficult installation areas may require extra planning and equipment.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you request a CCTV package quote.

Can I view cameras on my phone?

Yes. Mobile remote viewing setup can be included where the network and device support it.

Are cameras suitable for outdoors?

Outdoor camera suitability depends on camera type, weather rating and placement.

Is cabling included?

Standard cabling is included in package scope, while long or complex cable runs are quoted separately.

Can you maintain an existing CCTV system?

Yes. Once-off and monthly maintenance options are available for supported systems.

Do you install CCTV for remote sites?

Solar and 4G camera options can be quoted for remote sites where power or Wi-Fi is not available.

Can CCTV be combined with access control?

Yes. CCTV, access control, intercoms and networking can be planned as one security solution.
